## Part A: Case Study — UPI Payment Fraud

### UPI Fraud in India — A Growing Threat
UPI has revolutionized the way India transacts — fast, seamless, and available to anyone
with a smartphone. But this convenience has also made it one of the most exploited tools
by scammers. According to reports, India witnesses thousands of UPI fraud cases every day,
targeting people across all age groups, backgrounds, and education levels. College students
are particularly vulnerable as they are increasingly independent with their finances but
often unfamiliar with the tactics scammers use.

The most common UPI fraud methods include:
- **Fake payment requests** — sending a UPI request disguised as a payment
- **QR code scams** — asking victims to scan a code to "receive" money
- **Impersonation** — posing as bank officials, customer support, or buyers
- **OTP theft** — tricking users into sharing OTPs under the pretense of verification

### Staying Vigilant — How to Protect Yourself
The most important thing to remember is that **receiving money on UPI never requires
your PIN or OTP — ever.** Beyond that, staying safe comes down to a few simple but
critical habits:
- Always verify who you are transacting with before confirming any payment
- Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone over call, text, or chat
- Double check UPI IDs carefully — scammers use IDs that are nearly identical to real ones
- If something feels rushed or too good to be true, pause and verify before acting

## Part B: Stay Safe Online — Prevention Checklist

### Why Staying Safe Online Matters
The internet has made life significantly more convenient, but it has also opened the door
to a wide range of scams, frauds, and privacy violations that can cause serious harm.
Staying safe online is not just about protecting a password — it is about protecting your
money, your identity, your reputation, and your peace of mind.

Simple habits like using strong passwords, enabling two-factor authentication, and thinking
twice before clicking a link can be the difference between staying safe and becoming a
victim. Scammers rely on moments of unawareness and urgency — and awareness is the most
powerful tool against them.

### Why Reporting Cybercrimes Matters
Reporting a cybercrime is not just about recovering your own losses — it is an act that
protects others too. When a fraud is reported promptly on **cybercrime.gov.in** or via
helpline **1930**, authorities can act quickly to trace the scammer, freeze fraudulent
accounts, and potentially prevent the same person from targeting others. Every report
contributes to a larger picture that helps law enforcement identify patterns, shut down
fraud networks, and build safer digital infrastructure for everyone.

Many victims hesitate to report out of embarrassment or the assumption that nothing will
come of it — but prompt reporting significantly increases the chances of recovering lost
funds and brings accountability to those who exploit others online. Staying silent only
makes it easier for scammers to continue.
