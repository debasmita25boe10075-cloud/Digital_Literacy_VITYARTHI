 Stay Safe Online 
Report cybercrime at cybercrime.gov.in or call helpline 1930
 General Online Safety
1. Use strong, unique passwords for every account

   * Avoid using your name, birthday, or "1234"
   * Use a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols
   * Consider a password manager like Bitwarden (free)


   2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) everywhere
   * Especially on Gmail, Instagram, and any app linked to your bank
   * Prefer an authenticator app over SMS-based OTP when possible


   3. Never click on suspicious links — even from friends
   * Phishing links are often disguised as Google Forms, scholarship portals, or discount offers
   * Always check the URL before entering any personal information


   4. Don't use public Wi-Fi for sensitive activities
   * Avoid logging into banking apps or entering passwords on café or college Wi-Fi
   * Use your mobile data or a trusted VPN instead


   5. Regularly review app permissions on your phone
   * Many apps request access to contacts, camera, and location unnecessarily
   * Go to Settings → Apps → Permissions and revoke what isn't needed


   6. Be cautious about what you share on social media

      * Avoid posting your phone number, address, college ID, or daily schedule publicly
      * Scammers often use public profiles to build targeted fraud attempts
 Financial & UPI Safety (Must-Read)
      7. Never share your UPI PIN, OTP, or bank details with anyone — ever

         * Legitimate banks, Google Pay, PhonePe, or NPCI will never ask for your PIN
         * Receiving money on UPI does NOT require entering your PIN — if someone tells you otherwise, it's a scam


         8. Double-check UPI IDs and phone numbers before transferring money

            * Scammers create UPI IDs that look almost identical to real ones (e.g., support@icic1 instead of support@icici)
            * For any amount above ₹500, verify the receiver's name shown after entering the UPI ID before confirming


For emergencies
Report immediately at:
            * cybercrime.gov.in — File a complaint online for any cyber fraud
            *  Helpline 1930 — Call to report financial fraud and freeze transactions quickly
The faster you report financial fraud, the higher the chance of recovering your money.
